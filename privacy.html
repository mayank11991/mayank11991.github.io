Privacy Policy

Mantu: Kids Puzzle Games · Effective 16 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no chat.
  • Game progress stays on your device.
  • Players under 13 get child-directed ads only: no advertising ID and no personalised ads.
  • All ads are limited to G-rated, family-friendly content.

Who we are

This policy applies to the Mantu: Kids Puzzle Games app for Android (the "Application"), published by Mantu Games Studio (the "Service Provider", "we"). It explains what information the Application collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information the Application handles

  • Year of birth (stays on the device): when the Application is first opened it asks for the player's year of birth. This is stored only on the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. It is used only to choose the right ad settings, as described below.
  • Game data (stays on the device): player profiles (a nickname and an avatar picture), game progress, stars, coins and settings are stored only on the device.
  • Advertising data: the Application shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interactions and diagnostics to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. For players who say they are 13 or older, AdMob may also use the Advertising ID (a resettable identifier). For younger players, see "Children" below.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases (such as removing ads or buying coins) are processed by Google Play. The Application only receives confirmation that a purchase was completed; payment details are handled entirely by Google.
  • Crash log (stays on the device): if the Application closes unexpectedly, a short technical error log is kept on the device only. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Update check: the Application periodically downloads a small public file from this website to see whether a new version is available. No personal data is sent with that request.

Children

The Application is made for everyone, including children, and takes part in Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

  • If a player is under 13 (or the year of birth is not given), every ad request is tagged as child-directed. Google then does not use the Advertising ID, does not show personalised (interest-based) ads, and applies its rules for children's apps, in line with COPPA.
  • Players under 16 are also tagged as under the age of consent, so they are treated under GDPR's rules for minors.
  • For every player, the maximum ad content rating is set to G (suitable for all audiences).

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Application, contact us and we will help delete it.

How the information is used

  • To run the Application and keep each player's progress.
  • To show and measure ads through Google AdMob, and to prevent ad fraud.
  • To process and confirm in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • To let you know when a new version is available.

We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

The Application uses these services, which handle data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): how AdMob uses data and Google privacy policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent forms in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Google Play (purchases and in-app ratings): Google privacy policy.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the Application asks for consent before requesting ads and respects your choice. You can change it any time from Settings > Privacy choices in the Application.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • You can delete all data stored by the Application by clearing its data in your device settings or by uninstalling it.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the Application stores itself stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We do not keep a copy. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept by Google under its own policies. For any deletion request, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
