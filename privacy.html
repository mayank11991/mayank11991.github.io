Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Mantu app for mobile devices (the "Application"), together with any related services operated by info@khoj (the "Service Provider"). This policy explains what information the Application collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By downloading or using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

The Application does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or any personal contact information. The Application may collect the following information automatically:

  • Device and usage information: technical information about your device (such as operating system version and device identifiers), and information about how you use the Application, including gameplay statistics.
  • Advertising identifiers: the Application displays ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect the Advertising ID (a resettable identifier used for advertising) and related usage data to serve ads. Where required by law, your consent is collected before personalized ads are shown.
  • Purchase information: in-app purchases (such as removing ads or buying coins) are processed by Google Play. The Application receives only the confirmation that a purchase was completed; payment details are handled entirely by Google.

How We Use the Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Application and its features.
  • To serve and measure ads through Google AdMob, including personalized ads only where you have consented (in the European Economic Area, the UK, and other regions where consent is required, a consent form is shown before ads are requested).
  • To process and confirm in-app purchases made through Google Play.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process data as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): AdMob terms and Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play (in-app purchases): Google privacy policy.

Advertising and Your Choices

AdMob may use the Advertising ID to show you personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by resetting or restricting your Advertising ID in your device's settings. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the Application will ask for your consent before requesting ads, and will respect your choice. You can change your consent choices at any time in the Application's settings or your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for general audiences. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the Application's data in your device settings or uninstalling the Application. Advertising and analytics data is retained by the respective third-party providers according to their policies. If you wish to request deletion of any data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

The Service Provider may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date below will be updated. You are advised to review this page regularly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Service Provider at infoatkhoj@gmail.com.
